# About CamAnim

## The creator

Hi, I'm ElnullaIX! I'm Deaf and from France, and I made CamAnim. It was inspired by the original Camera Animation mod for VRChat, which disappeared when VRChat removed mods. I missed it so much that I decided to bring it back to life! ♡

## A big thank you

TachDeaf has helped me so much with English and with the tutorial videos. Here's her message:

> Hi, I'm TachDeaf! I'm Deaf and from Britain. I help ElnullaIX with English, and I write tutorials and explanations for the CamAnim website. _Note: I didn't create CamAnim, ElnullaIX did that herself._ ♡

Thank you also to Rollthenerd and Nestorboy for the "StreamCam" and "HideInCamera" shaders, and to @logi9_ for the VirtualLens2 guide.
