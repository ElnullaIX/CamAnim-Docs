# FAQ

## Who made CamAnim?

ElnullaIX! Read the story on the [About](./about.md) page. ♡

## Can I try it before I buy?

Yes! Use the [demo avatar](https://vrchat.com/home/avatar/avtr_89e2b4e1-7a6a-4dbc-9c06-dfef99ec0472). Open the link and click **Switch to Avatar**, or add it to your favourites and pick it in VRChat.

## How do I install it?

The short version:

1. Drag the **CamAnim** prefab onto your avatar.
2. Move **Hand Waypoints** next to your index fingertip.
3. Upload. Done! ♡

The full steps, with pictures, are in [Installation](./install/standard.md). The [Using in VRChat](./use.md) page explains every button.

## How do I link VRCLens with CamAnim?

Follow [Install with VRCLens](./install/vrclens.md). If it still doesn't work, check the DynVR animations: all the text should be yellow except one white line. Still stuck? Ask in **#general-help** on the [CamAnim Discord](https://discord.gg/KFPUU3pEKg) and ElnullaIX or TachDeaf will help.

## The camera doesn't appear, glitches, or keeps turning on and off

Set your VRC Camera behaviour to **Look at me**. CamAnim is linked to the VRChat camera, so the camera must look at your face.

![The VRC Camera behaviour set to Look at me](@site/static/img/LookAtMeBehaviour.webp)

## I see a silhouette in the focus layer when I use focus in the VRC Camera

That's a limit of the VRChat camera. For nice focus, link CamAnim with [VRCLens](./install/vrclens.md) or [VirtualLens2](./install/virtuallens2.md).

## VRCFury doesn't work

Update VRCFury to the latest version from the [VRCFury website](https://vrcfury.com/download). It works with Unity 2019 and Unity 2022. Prefer not to use VRCFury? Follow the [manual installation](./install/manual.md).

## Why doesn't it work on Quest?

Sorry! Quest doesn't support the Camera component that CamAnim needs. :(

## Can my friends see the paths I send from the desktop app?

Not exactly. VRChat only syncs these values in small steps (1/127), so other players would see the camera in slightly different places. That's why the desktop app works locally. [What is 1/127?](https://creators.vrchat.com/avatars/animator-parameters/#parameter-types)

## How far away can waypoints be saved?

Anywhere within 2 km × 2 km × 2 km in a world.
