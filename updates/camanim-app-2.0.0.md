The desktop app (formerly the OSC app) has a whole new look and a new name: **CamAnim**.

- New Library with a drawing of every path, plus search, rename, export and delete
- 3D editor: see your path, preview it, and fine-tune each waypoint live
- Recording with a live preview of your camera
- Compact mode: a small window that stays on top of VRChat
- A clear message when another app is already using the OSC port
- Camera rotation is recorded correctly again
- Lots of smaller fixes

Read the [desktop app guide](/docs/desktop-app).
